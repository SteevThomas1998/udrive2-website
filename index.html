    FAQs

    questions.

    Anything else? Call or text Mr. Farooq on 07843 449 667.

    How much do driving lessons cost?

    Pay-as-you-go lessons are £35 per hour. The Standard package is £340 for 10 hours, and the Intensive course is £680 for 20 hours of training.

    Do you pick me up from home?

    Yes — pick-up and drop-off is free within the local area. If you’re not sure whether you’re covered, get in touch and we’ll let you know.

    Can I have lessons in the evening or at weekends?

    Yes. Scheduling is flexible, with evening and weekend lessons available.

    What’s included in the Intensive course?
    • 20 hours of intensive training
    • Flexible scheduling
    • Mock test and pre-test assessment
    • Dedicated test preparation
    • Custom test date
    What do I need before my first lesson?

    You’ll need a valid UK provisional driving licence to drive on public roads. Bring it to your first lesson, along with comfortable, flat shoes.
