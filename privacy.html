Privacy notice

Your details, handled with care.

Last updated 17 November 2026

Who we are

UDrive2 School of Motoring provides driving lessons in Oldham and Ashton-under-Lyne, taught by Mr. Farooq. We are responsible for the personal information you share with us through this website, by phone or by text.

Questions about your information? Call or text 07843 449 667.

What we collect

When you send a lesson request through the website, we receive:

  • your name and mobile number
  • your postcode or area, if you give it (so we can arrange pick-up)
  • the type of lesson you’re interested in and the times that suit you

If you call or text us, we’ll also have your phone number and anything you tell us.

How we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and to arrange and deliver your lessons. We don’t sell your information, share it for others’ marketing, or send you marketing messages without your permission.

Our lawful basis under UK data protection law is that you’ve asked us to take steps before entering into an agreement with you (arranging lessons), and our legitimate interest in replying to enquiries.

How your request reaches us

Requests sent through the website form are delivered to us by email through a form-handling service, which processes the information only to pass it on to us. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts; these providers may log technical information such as your IP address for security and to serve the page. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your details only for as long as we need them to arrange and deliver your lessons and to meet any legal obligations, and then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Call or text 07843 449 667 to make a request.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
